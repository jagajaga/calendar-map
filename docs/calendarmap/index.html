Calendar Map

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026 · covers the Android and iPhone apps

module Privacy where

Calendar Map ("the app") is developed by Arseniy Seroka ("we", "us") and is open source. It shows events from the calendars on your phone on a map. We run no servers and receive no data from the app. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting. To draw maps, find places and plan routes, the app sends a small amount of data directly from your phone to the services listed below.

data read on your device

  • Calendar events (READ_CALENDAR): for events in the time range you pick, their title, start and end time, location, description, calendar name and colour. Shown in the app and not sent anywhere, except an event's location text as described below.
  • Your location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, only while the app is open): used on your device to show where you are, measure how far events are, and as the starting point of a route.

sent over the internet ≔ what → whom

WhatSent toWhen
An event's location text (an address or place name)The phone's geocoding service: on Android the built-in one (on most phones, Google); on iPhone, AppleThe first time that location is placed on the map; the result is cached on your device
Which map area you are viewing (map tile requests) and your IP addressAndroid: OpenStreetMap Foundation tile servers (tile.openstreetmap.org). iPhone: Apple MapsWhenever map tiles load
Coordinates of the events in a route, plus your location if the route starts from itOpenStreetMap routing service run by FOSSGIS e.V. (routing.openstreetmap.de)Only when you build a route

These services process the data to answer the request; their own policies apply: Google, Apple, OpenStreetMap Foundation, FOSSGIS. When you tap Open in Google Maps, Apple Maps or Calendar, that event's place or route is handed to the app you open, and that app's policy applies. All connections use HTTPS.

stored on your device

  • Your settings (time range, calendars, area mode, radius, route options)
  • A cache of place lookups (location text → coordinates)
  • A cache of map tiles

Kept in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android Settings deletes it.

what the app does NOT do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads, analytics, tracking or advertising ID
  • No crash reports or diagnostics sent to us or anyone else
  • No location access in the background
  • Never changes your calendar

children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date. If what the app sends changes, the app shows its in-app explanation again.
