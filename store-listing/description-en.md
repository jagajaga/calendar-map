# Calendar Map — Store Listing (English)

## App name (max 30 chars)
Calendar Map

## Short description (max 80 chars)
See your calendar events on a map and plan a route to attend as many as you can.

## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Lots of events this week and no idea which ones are close to each other?
Calendar Map puts every event from your calendars on a map, with its time
printed right on the pin.

**Your events, where they happen**
- Reads the calendars already on your phone (Google, Exchange, and more).
  No sign-in, nothing to set up.
- Every pin shows the event's title and its start and end time. Events at
  the same place share one pin.
- Shows the events in the part of the map you're looking at, or switch to
  "within X km of me" (default 50 km).
- Filter by time: today, tomorrow, the next 7 or 30 days, or any dates you
  pick.
- Choose which calendars to show.
- Tap a pin for the details. Open the place in Google Maps or the event in
  your calendar with one tap.

**Plan a route through your events**
Pick up to 10 events and Calendar Map works out which ones you can actually
make, and in what order. It respects every event's start and end time,
how long you want to stay at each (15 min, 30 min, 1 h, or the whole
event), and whether you're walking, cycling or driving.

You get:
- the road route on the map with numbered stops
- when to leave, and when to arrive at and leave each event
- your free time between events ("You have 2 h 30 min free to do whatever
  you want")
- the events that won't fit
- the whole route in Google Maps with one tap

Perfect for conference weeks, tech weeks, festivals, open-house days, or any
day with more plans than time.

**Private by design**
No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Your calendar stays on your phone. Only
event addresses (to find them on the map) and route coordinates (to plan a
route) are sent to the mapping services. See the privacy policy for details.
